Selected work.

    The hand behind the needle.

      From a
      conversation
      to skin.

      1. Conversation

        You tell Alexander what you are imagining: the idea, the feeling, the placement and the timing. The consultation below is the easiest way to start.

      2. Concept

        Your references, the shape of the body part and the style are worked into a direction. Size, flow and how the piece will age are part of the decision.

      3. Design

        An original drawing made for you, refined until it says exactly what was intended. No copies of other artists’ work.

      4. Tattoo session

        The stencil is placed and checked with you before the first line. Then the work, done carefully, with breaks whenever you need them.

      5. Healing & aftercare

        You leave with aftercare instructions. Follow them, look after the piece while it settles, and get in touch if anything concerns you.
