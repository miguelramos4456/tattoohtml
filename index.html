About the Artist

Alexander
Krane

With over a decade of experience shaping skin into narrative, Alexander Krane has built a reputation as one of Berlin’s most sought-after tattoo artists. His studio in Mitte has become a quiet sanctuary for those who seek art that endures long after the session ends.

Specialising in fine line realism and Japanese traditional, Alexander’s work balances delicate precision with bold compositional instinct. Each piece is developed through close collaboration — no two tattoos emerge from the same conversation, and no conversation is rushed.

Trained under master artists in Tokyo and Amsterdam, he brings a cross-cultural depth to his craft. Whether it’s a single brushstroke portrait or a full-sleeve composition rooted in irezumi tradition, every mark is intentional, every line considered.

Specialties
01

Fine Line Realism

Ultra-thin linework capturing photographic depth. Portraits, botanicals, and architectural detail rendered with sub-millimetre precision on any placement.

02

Japanese Traditional

Rooted in irezumi — bold outlines, flat fills, and timeless motifs. Dragons, koi, chrysanthemums, and waves executed with classical discipline and modern restraint.

03

Geometric & Blackwork

Sacred geometry, dotwork mandalas, and solid black fields. Pure form stripped of ornamentation — letting shape and negative space tell the story.

The Process
01

Consultation

We discuss your vision, placement, sizing, and skin tone. Every detail is considered before a single line is drawn or a design committed to paper.

02

Design

A custom design is crafted, refined through iteration until it speaks exactly the language you intended. No flash, no shortcuts — only originals.

03

Session

The studio is prepared. The stencil applied. The work begins — methodically, with full attention on every pass of the machine across your skin.

04

Aftercare

Detailed aftercare guidance ensures your piece heals correctly and retains its clarity, contrast, and definition for years to come.
